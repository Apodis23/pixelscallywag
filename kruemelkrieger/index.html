Wir erheben, speichern, übertragen oder teilen über die App keine personenbezogenen Daten. 2. Overview

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data through the app. There is no user account, no server, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking tools (e.g. no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Crashlytics) and no third-party software that collects data. The app works completely offline.

3. Data stored locally on your device

To make the app work, the following is stored only on your device: game progress (records, arcade progress, coins earned in the game, unlocked fighters and outfits), settings (sound, music, vibration, language, difficulty) and your touch-control layout. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Note: if you have enabled Android's automatic device backup (Google backup), Android may store this app data as part of your own device backup in your Google account. This is done by the operating system according to your settings and Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it. You can turn the backup off in Android settings.

4. Permissions

The app only requests the following Android permissions:

  • “Vibrate” (VIBRATE) – only for haptic feedback in the game; can be turned off in the app settings.

It has no internet permission and therefore cannot send any data. It has no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, calendar or your files.

Game controllers and keyboards are only used locally as input devices.

5. Google Play as distributor

The app is distributed via Google Play (provider in the EU: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When you view the store listing, download or update the app and – depending on your device settings – for crash and diagnostic reports, Google processes data under its own responsibility. At most we receive aggregated, non-personal statistics from Google (e.g. number of installs, crash rate). Details: Google's Privacy Policy.

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the message once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Children

Since the app does not collect any personal data, no such data is collected from children either.
